Policy via CRONIUM_SSH_HOST_KEY_POLICY (default accept-new; strict/insecure); trusted fingerprint persisted per server in the new servers.ssh_host_key column (migration 0010); mismatch fails closed. Mirrors the orchestrator's existing knownhosts model.
