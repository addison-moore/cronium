# 2026-11-06

- [2026-11-06] [Fix] **A database connection that stops answering no longer stalls the worker for good.** A stuck proxy, a failover or a firewall dropping packets closes nothing, so nothing ever errors. On the real worker, with old connections silent and new ones working, the dispatch, claim and outbox loops stayed stuck until a restart, and nothing restarts it: `restart: unless-stopped` doesn't act on an unhealthy container. The worker now closes any connection it has held for 5 minutes (`WORKER_DB_CHECKOUT_LIMIT_MS`) and asks Postgres to cancel what it was running. What was using it fails and rolls back, the pool slot comes back and the loop tries again. A schedule that came due during the stall follows its catch-up policy, as after any downtime. The pool and the `LISTEN` connection also use TCP keepalive, which notices a lost peer in about 40 seconds. This is not `query_timeout`, because that leaves the connection in use: when a slow statement outlasted it, the next transaction's `COMMIT` committed the work of the one that had failed. The `LISTEN` connection had the same problem, and with nothing to reconnect it, a 4-step workflow took 218 seconds instead of 12 until a restart. It now has its own timeouts and a check every 30 seconds (`WORKER_LISTEN_PROBE_INTERVAL_MS`). A worker started during a hang also never opened its health endpoint, and now does. `WORKER_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MS` is new too, in the same style as the other intervals. `tests/storage/hung-connection.test.ts` covers this on the real worker bundle.
