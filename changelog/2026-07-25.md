- [2026-07-25] [Bug Fix] **Workflow details page: trigger-type and schedule edits were silently dropped on save, and scheduled workflows rendered as "Manual" on load.** `WorkflowDetailsForm` kept a parallel local-state mirror for the trigger/schedule/override controls that never wrote back into react-hook-form (so the save payload used the untouched form values), and the mirror was seeded with hardcoded defaults before the form reset (so the stored SCHEDULE trigger/schedule section didn't render until any field changed). Two RTL regression tests pin the fixed behaviors (UI schedule edits reach the `workflows.update` payload; schedule section renders on first paint). Found by the Phase 6 UI safety net.
