- [2026-07-17] [Refactor] Removed the Conditional Actions feature (see `_plans/conditional-actions/`). Workflows already cover the same branching — on-success / on-failure / on-condition edges, fan-out, scheduling, and both "run another event" and "send a notification" — and the two features silently compounded (an event used as a workflow step fired its own conditional actions in addition to the workflow's edges).
